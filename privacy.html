NEXUS privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

Who uses NEXUS

NEXUS is a self-hosted personal assistant with exactly one user: its owner, who also operates it. It is not a public service and does not accept sign-ups.

Google data it accesses

  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly, calendar.events): reads the owner's events to build reminders and the morning brief; creates or deletes an event only after the owner approves that specific action.
  • Gmail (gmail.readonly): reads message headers, snippets and, when the owner asks, message text, to alert the owner about mail from senders the owner marked as important and to answer the owner's questions about their mail. NEXUS cannot send, modify or delete email.

Where the data goes

  • Google data is processed on the owner's own computer. The language model runs locally on that computer.
  • A local database on that computer stores a cache of calendar events and the alerts NEXUS creates (for an email alert: sender name, subject and a short snippet).
  • Alerts and replies are delivered to the owner's own Telegram chat and, as a backup, to the owner's private ntfy push topic. These services therefore carry the alert text.
  • Google data is not sold, not shared with anyone else, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

NEXUS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the local database at any time. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; NEXUS then stops reading Google data.

Contact

NEXUS is operated by its single user. Questions about this page: open an issue in this repository.
